◆ sgmt_initial_guess_points()

template<typename Drivetrain , typename Solution >
void trajopt::PathBuilder< Drivetrain, Solution >::sgmt_initial_guess_points ( size_t  from_index,
std::span< const Pose2d >  guess_points 
)
inline

Adds a sequence of initial guess points between two waypoints.

The points are inserted between the waypoints at fromIndex and fromIndex + 1. Linear interpolation between the waypoint initial guess points and these segment initial guess points is used as the initial guess of the robot's pose over the trajectory.

Parameters
from_indexThe index of the waypoint the initial guess point comes after.
guess_pointsThe sequence of initial guess points.
